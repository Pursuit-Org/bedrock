Account coverage audit

Who's missing from Operation 35 — Pursuit

The Pursuit set is meant to hold every firm we'd prioritise on warmth and strength of relationship. Salesforce and Bedrock agree it already has the big hirers. What it doesn't have is a third of our commit partners, our largest donors who've never been asked to hire, and almost every frontier-AI and VC account we talk to weekly.

What the data says

Five findings

  1. The "hired 5+ builders" tranche is already comprehensive

    Of the 30 Salesforce accounts with five or more recorded hires, only three are absent — and none is actionable. Intrepid Pursuits (6 hires) wound down; AWS (6 hires) is covered through Amazon, whose tagged contact David Ham in fact sits in AWS Global Services; DeepMile (5 hires) is a Comcast subsidiary and Comcast is covered. No action needed on this dimension.

  2. Eight of twenty-one commit partners are missing

    iCapital, SeatGeek, Cedar, Spring Health, Ballistic Ventures, David Energy, Foursquare and Quizlet have no tagged contact. Skillshare is nominally covered, but only by a volunteer-level contact with no title on file — thin for a commit partner.

  3. The real gap sits at two-to-four hires plus an unconverted relationship

    These are firms that have already proven they'll hire from us and carry an active giving or PBD relationship nobody has turned into a hiring channel. Deutsche Bank ($925k given, 89 activities, zero hires), Salesforce ($1.05M, PBD portfolio), Alphadyne ($1.18M, CTO already in the thread), MetLife ($551k) and Mizuho (172 activities and a live jobs opportunity) are the clearest cases.

  4. Frontier AI and VC/PE are systematically under-represented

    Anthropic, OpenAI, Stripe, a16z, Union Square Ventures, Apollo, KKR, Silver Lake, General Atlantic and AlleyCorp all sit in the 2026 PBD portfolio or carry heavy recent activity, and none is in the Pursuit set. For the funds the value is the portfolio-hiring channel rather than direct headcount.

  5. Five accounts we can't tag yet, because there's nobody senior to tag

    Barclays (4 hires), Meta (4 hires), T-Mobile (4 hires) and Palo Alto Networks (83 activities, $201k) have no senior contact on file. BlackRock is the starkest: a $2.27M lifetime donor on Strategic tier and in the PBD portfolio, whose most senior contact in the CRM is a Vice President. These need sourcing before tagging is meaningful.

Method warning

High activity is not the same as a hiring prospect

Ranking accounts purely on logged activity volume surfaces Pursuit's own vendors, not employers. Each of these was checked against the actual email participants and excluded from the recommendations.

Justworks is the single most active account in the CRM — 601 activities in eighteen months — because it is our PEO and 832 of its 1,322 emails come from support@justworks.com. The same pattern holds for Newmark and Cushman & Wakefield (real-estate brokers), Wachtell Lipton and Cahill Gordon (outside counsel), Russell Reynolds (executive search working for us), Powered by Professionals (event production), VVA (construction management), Bolton St. Johns and Hudson Ferris (lobbying and fundraising consultants), and Acrisure (insurance broker).

Together they account for well over a thousand logged activities. Recommendation: add a vendor flag to Salesforce accounts, or this will keep polluting every warmth-ranked list we build.

Before this list is used again

Data problems worth fixing

  • Two sources of truth for "hires." Salesforce reads Uber 35, Citi 49, Blackstone 5 — against the 47, 17 and 18 in the commit-partner list. Reconcile before either number drives targeting.
  • The jobs pipeline can't be joined to Salesforce. bedrock.jobs_account.sf_account_id is null for all 277 rows, so every match had to fall back to name strings.
  • Duplicate accounts split the signal. Citi/Citigroup, Verizon ×2, Mastercard ×2, Poll Everywhere ×2, MetLife/MetLife Foundation each divide hire counts, giving totals and activity across records.
  • Contact links are incomplete. Tagged contacts at Moody's, Thumbtack, Goldman Sachs, JPMC, NYTimes, OpenRouter, Kisi and TPG have no sf_contact_link row, so coverage had to be resolved by name matching as well as by ID.
  • No vendor flag exists. See the method warning above.
